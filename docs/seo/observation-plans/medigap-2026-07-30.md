**Window methodology — the one trap to avoid.** The baseline
(`pages-2026-07-29.csv`) is a ~3-month rolling window. A fresh 3-month export
taken two weeks after the change is still ~85% pre-change data and will dilute
any real movement toward the baseline value. Therefore:

- The **engine** keeps running on the standard rolling export (queue health,
  site-wide reports).
- The **experiment judgment** uses a *post-change-only* custom date range
  export (2026-07-31 → present), compared against the baseline values. Do not
  judge position from a window that mixes pre- and post-change days.
- A post-change window needs **≥14 days and ≥30 impressions** before its
  average position is worth reading at all; the 4-week decision point is the
  earliest legitimate verdict.

## 3. Success thresholds

**Primary hypothesis (average position):**

| Verdict | Criteria (post-change-only window, ≥30 impressions) |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | Average position ≤ 10.0 (top-10 entry from 13.73) |
| **Directionally supported** | Improvement ≥ 2.0 positions (≤ 11.7) but not top-10 |
| **Not supported** | Movement < 2.0 positions in either direction after 8 weeks |
| **Regressed** | Position worsens ≥ 2.0 positions, or impressions fall >30% while site-wide impressions are flat/up |

**Secondary observations:**

- **Impressions** — supported if the post-change window, annualized against
  the baseline window length, is flat or up. Depth should widen query match;
  a material decline is the regression guard above.
- **CTR / clicks** — recorded, **not judged**. At ~70 impressions/quarter,
  expected clicks ≈ 1; no CTR delta on this page is attributable this cycle.
  The title/meta rewrite shipped with the bundle but its evaluation is
  deferred until the page (or the site) reaches the CTR-confidence bar
  (~10 pages with ≥10 clicks).
- **Engine flags** — `thin` and `aiGap` should clear on the next run by
  construction; `strikingDistance` clearing would itself be evidence of
  top-5 or >15 movement (check direction before celebrating).

## 4. What this experiment can and cannot conclude

This optimization exercised **seven patterns simultaneously**: comparison
table, definitions block, source section, internal-link expansion (6→12),
contextual depth/prose (rural Utah + enrollment guidance), FAQ expansion, and
title/meta rewrite. Dynamic figures from `annualMedicareData` are an
implementation technique inside the table/prose patterns, not a separate
ranking pattern.

**The page validates the bundle, not each component.**

Can conclude (if supported): applying the full editorial standard to a thin,
striking-distance pillar improves average position — evidence *for the bundle
on this page archetype* (thin pillar, strong inbound authority, pos 5–15).

Cannot conclude: which individual pattern drove the movement; that any single
pattern deserves Proven status; that the result generalizes beyond this
archetype; anything about CTR or the title/meta rewrite. A single supported
outcome advances every **manipulated** pattern's evidence column by exactly one
bundled data point — no more, and it advances no other pattern at all.

**Attribution boundary** (Pattern Ledger attribution rule,
`docs/seo/WORK-LOG.md`): of the six ledger rows, this experiment may credit
**four** — comparison table, definitions, sources, internal-link pattern. The
other three manipulated patterns (contextual depth, FAQ expansion, title/meta)
are judged inside the bundle but have no ledger row. The **AI summary and CTA
rows receive nothing**: `/medigap` already carried both before this change
(baseline §3, "Present") and the hypothesis concerned neither. Evidence follows
intervention, not page inventory.

**Design consequence for later Sprint 2B pages:** where the queue's
prescription is naturally narrow (e.g. a single missing element), prefer the
minimal change over the full bundle, so the Pattern Ledger accumulates
isolated evidence. Full-bundle rewrites remain appropriate for pages failing
most of the standard at once.

## 5. Graduation criteria (per the Scientific Rule, `docs/ROADMAP.md`)

**Experimental → Proven** requires *all* of:

1. Supported (or directionally supported) outcomes on **≥3 pages across ≥2
   optimization cycles** where the pattern was present, recorded as Outcome
   entries in WORK-LOG.md citing DELTA.md evidence.
2. At least **one** of those outcomes from a change where the pattern was
   applied in isolation or in a ≤2-pattern change — pure-bundle evidence
   alone cannot promote a single pattern.
3. No regressed outcome attributable to the pattern.

**Proven → Standard** additionally requires:

1. A deliberate edit to `docs/EDITORIAL-STANDARD.md` (its "Changing the
   standard" procedure), never automatic promotion.
2. Gate enforcement added (`scripts/seo/gate.mjs` / standards tests), so the
   requirement is checked, not aspirational.
3. Evidence the pattern holds across **at least two content archetypes**
   (e.g. pillar + spoke), since Standard binds all future content.

Current ledger status: all six patterns remain **Experimental**. A supported
/medigap outcome moves **four** evidence columns — comparison table,
definitions, sources, internal-link pattern — to "1 bundled support
(EXP-001, /medigap, Sprint 2B #1)", still Experimental. The AI summary and CTA
rows stay at `—`; see the attribution boundary in §4.
