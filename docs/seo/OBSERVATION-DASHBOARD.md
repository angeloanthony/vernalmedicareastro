# Sprint 2B Observation Dashboard

**Read-only operational view of every in-flight experiment.** Nothing here is
original: every field is derived from `docs/seo/EXPERIMENTS.md`, the immutable
baselines, and the observation plans. This file introduces **no hypotheses, no
baseline changes, and no outcome evaluation**. Its only job is to let anyone
see, on one screen, what is being observed, when the next check falls due, and
what would invalidate a reading — while the experiments are still in flight.

Not to be confused with `docs/seo/DASHBOARD.md`, which is machine-generated by
`npm run seo` and reports site state, not experiment state.

> **Compiled 2026-07-30** from the sources listed per experiment; re-derived
> the same day at the Sprint 2B governance freeze
> (`docs/seo/SPRINT-2B-FREEZE.md`), which added the attribution rule and the
> per-experiment *Ledger rows creditable* field. Update it by re-deriving from
> those documents after each check; never by reasoning here.

**Rules for this file**

- Derived only. If a fact is not in a baseline, plan, or registry, it does not
  belong here.
- No verdicts. Verdicts are written at Delta Review by filling baseline §8 and
  the WORK-LOG Outcome — never in a dashboard.
- No early reads. Positions and impressions are deliberately absent from this
  file until each experiment reaches its decision point.

**How the dates are derived.** All three plans specify: recrawl confirmation
~1 week after deploy, decision point no earlier than 4 weeks of *post-recrawl*
data, hard stop 8 weeks post-recrawl. Taking recrawl confirmation on
2026-08-06, the earliest legitimate Delta Review is **2026-09-03** and the hard
stop is **2026-10-01** — for all three. Both dates slip one week for every week
a page is not recrawled; the plans require pushing the check out and requesting
indexing rather than judging early.

**Shared window methodology** (all three plans, identical): the verdict uses a
**post-change-only** GSC export (2026-07-31 → present) compared against the
baseline. A fresh 3-month rolling export is mostly pre-change data and dilutes
real movement toward the baseline; it stays in use for queue health only. A
post-change window needs ≥14 days and ≥30 impressions before its average
position is readable at all.

**Shared environment:** Performance Mode ON, verdict "confirmed,
low-confidence CTR" (`docs/PERFORMANCE-VALIDATION-2026-07-30.md`). CTR and
clicks are **recorded, not judged** in all three experiments. Promotion of CTR
to a judged metric requires an explicit observation-log entry made *before* the
data is read.

**Shared attribution rule** (canonical text in the Pattern Ledger,
`docs/seo/WORK-LOG.md`; adopted 2026-07-30): at Delta Review, evidence is
credited **only to the ledger rows an experiment intentionally manipulated** —
listed per experiment below as *Ledger rows creditable*. Patterns already
present on the page are environment, not evidence. The set is fixed at baseline
time and cannot be widened after the data is read.

## Cross-experiment engine-model findings

Carried here so every observation check can be read against the same set of
known engine behaviours. Each is already recorded in the document cited; this
is an index, not a new claim. A dedicated `ENGINE-VALIDATION.md` is
**deliberately deferred until EXP-003 reaches Closed**, so it can start from a
stable set of observations rather than one still accruing follow-up checks.

| # | Finding | Consequence for observation | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `normalizeUrl()` folds extensionless URLs to `.html` variants | GSC rows match page rows; pre-fix position figures are not comparable | WORK-LOG 2026-07-30 (engine change, no EXP ID) |
| 2 | The `aiGap` floor is **70**, not 67 | A page at 67 keeps the flag; clearing it needs further AI work | EXP-002 plan §0 erratum 1 |
| 3 | Freshness reads **git commit dates** of a route's `sourceFiles` only | Component/i18n edits are invisible to freshness; uncommitted edits are invisible entirely | EXP-002 plan §0 erratum 2 · EXP-003 erratum 3 |
| 4 | Freshness is a **staleness** factor (`clamp01(ageDays/365)`) — older scores higher | A refresh *removes* ~0.4 Priority points; it can never add 4–5 | EXP-002 plan §0 erratum 3 |
| 5 | AI readiness is **not page-local**: `insights` scores `words >= medianWords`, and the median is site-relative and recomputed every run | The 12-page edit moved the median 552 → 566 and cost three untouched pages 3.3 AI points each; 46 EN pages sit within ±60 words of it. Site-wide AI movement between runs is not the sum of page edits. `/medicare-formulary-lookup` at AI 65 is an artifact of EXP-003, not a page regression | EXP-003 plan §0 errata 1–2 |
| 6 | `hasComparisonTable` is pure shape (`cols >= 2 && rows >= 3`) | Structural presence, not semantic quality — a degenerate table and a genuinely comparative one score identically. Only editorial review distinguishes them | EXP-003 plan §0 erratum 4 |

Items 3 and 5 are the two that can corrupt a Delta reading if forgotten; item 6
cannot, but it bounds what a completeness or AI score is evidence *of*.
