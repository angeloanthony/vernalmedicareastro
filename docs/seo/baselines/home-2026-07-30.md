# Page Baseline Report — / (home)

**Status: IMMUTABLE BASELINE.** This file records the state of `/` *before*
its Sprint 2B optimization. Do not edit after the optimization begins; the
first Delta review compares against these numbers.

**Experiment role: first isolated editorial-pattern experiment.** The Medigap
experiment (Sprint 2B #1) changed a seven-pattern bundle; a supported outcome
there validates the bundle, not any component. This experiment exists to
generate the Pattern Ledger's first **isolated** evidence: exactly one
pattern — the Standard AI summary (`<SummaryBlock>`) — applied to exactly one
page. If performance moves, the evidence attributes to that single
intervention. The two experiments are complementary: bundle evidence for the
editorial standard as a whole, isolated evidence for one pattern within it.

individual query. This is why impressions must be read alongside position at
every check, and why single-query watch rows are orientation only.

## 6. Risk assessment — what would invalidate attribution

The homepage is the site's most-connected, most-tempting-to-edit page. These
are the identified confounders, with the response to each:

1. **Shared component with `/es.html`.** The edit ships to both routes. The
   experiment is judged on `/` (EN) only; `/es.html` movement is recorded as
   orientation. Not a confound so long as the ES copy is the translation of
   the same block and nothing more.
2. **The NewsTicker is data-driven** (`src/data/news.ts` → up to 8 headlines,
   featured-first). Publishing, retiring, or re-featuring *any* news article
   changes homepage body content and outbound links automatically, mid-
   experiment, with no homepage commit. This is the largest confound risk.
   Response: news operations are not frozen (unrealistic), but every
   `news.ts` change during the window must be noted in the observation log.
   A position move that coincides with a large ticker change is downgraded
   to "directional at best."
3. **Sitewide template changes.** The homepage inherits BaseLayout, nav
   (M44 dropdowns), and `styles.css` like every page — but as the depth-0,
   highest-volume page it feels template changes most. Any nav/layout/schema
   change during the window is a confound; log it.
4. **Inbound-link drift from other Sprint 2B work.** Other queue
   optimizations may add links *to* the homepage (inbound currently 10).
   Avoid doing so during the window; if a queue prescription demands it,
   log it.
5. **Auto-commit tooling.** This environment auto-commits WIP to main. Before
   the delta review, check `git log -- src/components/content/HomePage.astro
   src/i18n/content/*/home* src/data/news.ts` for the window and reconcile
   every commit against this scope statement.
6. **Query-mix sensitivity** (§1): with 1,040 impressions across many
   queries, average position can move without ranking changes. Judged only
   with impressions alongside, on a post-change-only window, per the
   /medigap methodology (`docs/seo/observation-plans/medigap-2026-07-30.md`
   §1) — a fresh rolling export dilutes ~against the baseline and must not
   be used for the verdict.

**Attribution rule:** if a §6 confound of type 2–5 materially fires,
the outcome is capped at *directionally supported* and the Pattern Ledger
entry is recorded as "supported with confound noted," which does **not**
satisfy the isolated-evidence requirement for graduation. Protecting this
experiment's isolation is worth more than any individual homepage
improvement idea — park them in the queue.

## 7. Optimization Hypothesis

> **We expect this change to improve average position because the homepage
> already ranks at 7.2 in the striking-distance band on the site's highest
> impression volume, and its dominant structural deficit is extractability
> (AI readiness 47, `needsSummary`) — adding the standard AI summary block
> under the H1 gives Google and AI surfaces a clean, quotable statement of
> what the page is about, which is the single element the queue prescribes.**

Primary metric: **average position** (post-change-only window, judged
against 7.15). Secondary: impressions (guard — should hold or rise).
**CTR and clicks: observed, not judged** unless the site reaches the
CTR-confidence bar (~10 pages with ≥10 clicks) during the window. The
homepage's 23 clicks make it the page *closest* to meaningful CTR data, but
per `docs/PERFORMANCE-VALIDATION-2026-07-30.md` the environment verdict is
"confirmed, low-confidence CTR" — one page does not clear the bar. If the
bar is reached mid-experiment, CTR may be promoted to a judged secondary
metric by an explicit observation-log entry, never retroactively.

Copy this hypothesis into `docs/seo/WORK-LOG.md` as a new entry **when the
edit begins** — not before. Pattern exercised: **Standard AI summary** (one
pattern, ledger row 3). A supported outcome here is the ledger's first
isolated data point and, combined with a supported /medigap bundle outcome,
would put the AI-summary pattern at 2 data points (1 isolated + 1 bundled) —
the isolated one satisfying graduation criterion #2.
